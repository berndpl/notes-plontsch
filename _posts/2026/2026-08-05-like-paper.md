What screens do and how we relate to them is changing and when I think of something that might help guide us along that unbeaten path it is paper.

A blank sheet of paper can become almost anything. It is easy to pick up and do something with it. At the same time it's an incredibly versatile canvas for expression, a tool for communication and medium for preserving thought over time. Paper combines accessibility and versatility effortlessly.

Screen-based devices have approached this ideal in theory, but the cost of creating and changing software has kept authorship scarce and creators exclusive. Now, an idea and the will to manifest is enough. Minds blown.

## The Truly Personal Computer

The new idea of a truly personal computer isn't just everybody being able to use and leverage computers anymore but also everybody being able to fully shape it in its entirety around their individual needs. The software user relationship becomes one to one.

## Digital Dopamine

It could feel counter intuitive but that same abundance might also help resolve our crisis of digital stimulation. When screens are become everything all at once, we just grow tired of that rush of flashing pixels eventually.

Screens might slowly become boring enough again to allow us turn inward and becoming more aware again. In that world, screens would become more like paper: A nearly invisible enabler for doing things, rather than toys we endlessly fidget with and just can't seem to let go of.
