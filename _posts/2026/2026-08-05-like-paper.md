What screens do and how we relate to them is changing and when I think of something that might help guide us along that unbeaten path it is paper.

A blank sheet of paper can be almost anything. It is so easy to pick up and do something with it. At the same time it's an incredibly versatile canvas for expression, a tool for communication and even a medium for preserving thought over time. Paper combines accessibility and versatility effortlessly.

Screen-based devices have approached this ideal in theory, but the cost of creating and changing software has kept authorship scarce and creators exclusive. Making what happens on a screen feel special. Now, an idea and the will to manifest is enough. Minds blown.

## The Truly Personal Computer

The new idea of a personal computer isn't just everybody being able to use computers but to fully shape it in its entirety around your individual needs. The software user relationship becomes one to one.

## Overcoming the Digital Dopamine

It could feel counter intuitive but my hope is that same abundance might also help resolve our crisis of digital over stimulation. When screens become everything all at once, we might just grow tired of that rush of flashing pixels and opening up space to turn inward, becoming more aware of ourselves and the people around us. 

A nearly invisible enabler for doing things, rather than toys we endlessly fidget with and just can't seem to let go of. In that world, screens become paper.
